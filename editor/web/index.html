Position

Timing (seconds)

Shapes

A coloured shape for a time window: a backing under text, a bar, a frame or a colour field. Place text above it by giving the text a higher track. Drag it on the preview, mouse wheel resizes it.

Scenes

A scene groups texts, shapes, videos on top and sounds. Mark a range (I and O) or put the playhead on the first item, then bundle. Drag the scene on its lane and everything inside moves with it.

Video on top (picture-in-picture)

A second video track above the main picture: a reaction, a logo clip, a b-roll. Move it on the OVERLAY lane, drag its edges to trim, drag it on the preview to place it.

Loudness

-14 LUFS suits Reels, TikTok, Shorts and YouTube. Use -16 for podcasts.

Audio track (music, voice-over)

Any audio file (or a video with sound). Move it on the audio lane, drag its edges to trim.

The preview plays the sound but not the ducking; the export applies it. Boosts above 0 dB are only heard in the export.
